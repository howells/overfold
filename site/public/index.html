Overfold

A quieter menu bar.

Overfold hides the menu bar icons you don't need, and brings them back with a click.

Download for Mac

Free. macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

Always shown

Right of the grey line. Wi-Fi, battery, the clock, and anything you glance at all day.

Shown on click

Between the lines. Click the arrow or press ⌃⌥O. They tuck away when you click elsewhere, or after 15 seconds, but never while a menu is open.

Kept out of sight

Left of the blue line. Sync clients and agents that never need you. ⌥-click the arrow to reach them.

“Hold ⌘ and drag. That's the setup.”

No settings window, no permissions, nothing to subscribe to. One tiny app that starts at login and stays out of the way.

Set in Newsreader and San Francisco. Free and open source under the MIT licence.
